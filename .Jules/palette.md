## 2026-07-10 - Ensuring Resource Connectivity in Headless APIs
**Learning:** In purely backend or headless applications, the "User Interface" is the API, and "Developer Experience (DX)" is the primary form of UX. When consumers execute long-running or asynchronous tasks (such as importing a resource), returning only the operation status is insufficient. Consumers need the exact identifier of the resulting entity to seamlessly transition to the next step (e.g. fetching or rendering the imported receipt). Populating the `receipt_id` on both new and idempotent/duplicate runs is a crucial DX improvement that enables effortless client-side navigation.
**Action:** When designing asynchronous API endpoints, always ensure that the terminal success response contains the unique identifier of the produced or processed entity, even when the operation was a deduplicated/idempotent run.
