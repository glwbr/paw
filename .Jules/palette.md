## 2026-07-02 - [API Hypermedia and Failure-Fast Validation]
**Learning:** In headless projects, UX is Developer Experience (DX). Discoverability through hypermedia (like captcha_url) and immediate validation (failure-fast) are the "UI delights" of an API.
**Action:** Always look for multi-step processes that can be guided by links in the response, and validate identifiers early to provide actionable feedback.
