## 2026-07-06 - [Hypermedia Discoverability in Headless APIs]
**Learning:** In backend-only/headless projects, UX is synonymous with Developer Experience (DX). Providing resource-specific hypermedia links (like `captcha_url`) during asynchronous or multi-step processes significantly improves discoverability and reduces the cognitive load on API consumers.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to guide API consumers through complex state transitions by including relevant links or "next step" metadata in the resource representation.
