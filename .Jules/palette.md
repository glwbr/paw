## 2026-07-06 - [Hypermedia in Headless APIs]
**Learning:** In headless projects, UX is Developer Experience (DX). Providing resource-specific hypermedia links (like `captcha_url`) to guide consumers through multi-step asynchronous processes significantly improves API discoverability and reduces client-side implementation effort.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to provide direct links to the next required action in asynchronous workflows.
