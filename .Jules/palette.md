## 2025-05-14 - Hypermedia links and failure-fast validation in headless projects
**Learning:** In backend-only projects, DX/UX quality is measured by actionable error messages and discoverability via hypermedia links. These guide consumers through multi-step asynchronous processes and provide immediate feedback.
**Action:** Provide resource-specific links (like `captcha_url`) to guide consumers and validate inputs (like `access_key`) as early as possible in the API layer.
