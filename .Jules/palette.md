## 2026-06-25 - API Discoverability and Fail-Fast Validation
**Learning:** In headless projects, UX is defined by the API's discoverability and feedback loop. Providing hypermedia links (like `captcha_url`) and immediate validation of complex identifiers (like 44-digit access keys) significantly improves the developer and end-user experience.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to add resource-specific links to guide clients through multi-step processes and validate inputs at the earliest possible entry point.
